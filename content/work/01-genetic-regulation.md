<p>I have led research into the genetic and molecular mechanisms that control plant form, encompassing flower morphology, wood development, leaf architecture, and phyllotactic patterning. My approach integrates wet-lab molecular biology with high-throughput computational phenotyping, enabling quantitative analysis of developmental processes at scale.</p>
