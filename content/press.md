title: "Press"
---

My research and public scholarship have been featured in national and international outlets including *Nature*, *Smithsonian Magazine*, *National Geographic*, and *Mozilla*. Below is a selection of press coverage, interviews, and articles in which I contributed expertise, was recognized for my work, or where coverage highlights research from teams I have led.

---

[**Global Plastics AI Policy Tool — UN Treaty Coverage**](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/11/24/climate/plastic-pollution-south-korea-talks.html) — *The New York Times*, 2024 · Research on pathways to reduce global plastic waste, also covered by [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2024/nov/14/global-plastic-production-cut-pollution-waste-un-treaty), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/11/25/nx-s1-5199658/plastic-pollution-un-treaty-fossil-fuel-climate-change-health), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/plastic-treaty-talks-near-study-shows-how-cut-waste-clogging-oceans-1985919), and [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/interactive/2023/plastic-pollution-united-nations-agreement-science-recycle/).

[**How to make your scientific data accessible, discoverable and useful**](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-01929-7) — *Nature*, 2023 · Interviewed as part of a group of specialists offering tips for effectively sharing scientific data.

[**Behind the Scenes of "The Future of Data Science": An Interview with Ciera Martinez**](https://nightingaledvs.com/behind-the-scenes-of-the-future-of-data-science-an-interview-with-ciera-martinez/) — *Nightingale Magazine*, 2022 · Interview about my work leading the creation of Data Science by Design's first book.

[**150 Years Celebration of Women in Computing at Berkeley**](https://data.berkeley.edu/people/ciera-martinez) — *UC Berkeley*, 2022 · A profile on how I came to be a data scientist researcher and my current work at BIDS.

[**How to approach accessibility in academic data science**](https://academicdatascience.org/resources/2021-09-23-blog) — *Academic Data Science Alliance*, 2021 · Summary of a group discussion with BIDS's Best Practices in Data Science group on data accessibility.

[**The Gateway at UC Berkeley: Lighting the Way to Prevent California Wildfires**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJa8UJ-udu8&ab_channel=UCBerkeley%3ALighttheWay) — *UC Berkeley*, 2021 · Short film about my background, research, and the future of UC Berkeley's Division for Computing, Data Science and Society.

[**100 Inspiring Hispanic/Latinx Scientists in America**](http://crosstalk.cell.com/blog/100-inspiring-hispanic-latinx-scientists-in-america) — *Cell Crosstalk*, 2020 · A list including myself and 99 other fantastic Latinx scientists.

[**Decoding the Secrets of Plants' Stunning Leaf Patterns**](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/decoding-mathematical-secrets-plants-stunning-leaf-patterns-180972367/) — *Smithsonian Magazine*, 2019 · Interviewed about statistical modeling in plant sciences in the context of a study on phyllotactic patterning.

[**11 Ways to Avert a Data-storage Disaster**](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-019-01040-w) — *Nature*, 2019 · Interviewed and quoted on data management techniques and strategies.

[**Watch These Flies Turn Into Zombies**](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/2018/08/zombie-flies-mind-control-fungus-animals/) — *National Geographic*, 2018 · Write-up of work I was part of in Michael Eisen's lab about zombie fruit fly fungus.

[**The State of Open Science and What's Ahead**](https://medium.com/read-write-participate/the-state-of-open-science-and-whats-ahead-ce2718689281) — *Medium*, 2018 · Describes the work of myself and five other Mozilla Open Science Fellows.

[**Mozilla Announces 25 New Fellows in Openness, Science, and Tech Policy**](https://blog.mozilla.org/blog/2018/08/21/mozilla-announces-25-new-fellows-in-openness-science-and-tech-policy/) — *Mozilla*, 2018 · Introduction to the 2018/2019 Mozilla Fellows Cohort.

[**Rule Rewrite Aims to Clean up Scientific Software**](https://www.nature.com/news/rule-rewrite-aims-to-clean-up-scientific-software-1.17323) — *Nature*, 2015 · Interviewed and quoted asking peer reviewers to check accessibility of code used in computational studies.

[**Is Your Leaf Left Handed?**](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2012/06/120623094318.htm) — *Science Daily*, 2012 · Describes work I was part of using mathematical modeling and morphometrics to describe phyllotactic patterning in plants.
