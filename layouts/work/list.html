Program Leadership

As Senior Program Manager at the Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center for Data Science and Environment, I developed and led four programmatic areas — each combining original research, tool development, policy engagement, and cross-sector partnership.

Open & Democratize Geospatial Data

Co-launched GeoJupyter to make geospatial data analysis accessible through open-source tools and Jupyter's collaborative infrastructure. Led user research (40+ hours of interviews), organized hackathons and workshops, and supported researchers publishing with the platform.

JupyterGIS Open Source Geospatial

Reduce the Worst Greenhouse Gases

Built Kigali Sim in partnership with the United Nations — a free, open-source modeling tool helping policymakers optimize HFC emissions reductions under the Montreal Protocol. Beta tested with 12+ countries and NGOs; officially launched December 2025. Estimated potential: 5% GHG reduction in middle-income nations by 2040.

UN Partnership Climate Policy Open Source

End Plastic Pollution

Developed AI-powered policy tools and predictive models to support UN plastic treaty negotiations. Research published in Science demonstrated that four coordinated policies could reduce mismanaged plastic waste by 91% by 2050. Briefed delegations globally and presented at the World Economic Forum and New York Climate Week.

UN Treaty AI Policy Tools Published in Science

Co-Design Environmental Stewardship

Partnered with Indigenous and local communities to co-design land management tools that integrate traditional ecological knowledge with modern data science. Built software with the Karuk Wildlife Team, expanded Soundhub for bioacoustic AI monitoring across 80 Sentinel Sites, and advanced frameworks for Indigenous data sovereignty.

Indigenous Partnership Data Sovereignty Biodiversity
